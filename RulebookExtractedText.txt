GROUP-WISE EVENT STRUCTURE
The 2026 participation structure follows the event
grouping provided in the Abhivyakti Mastersheet.

=== PAGE 4 ===
Eligibility: All current students of participating
colleges, with valid institutional identification, are
eligible to participate.
Maximum Events Allowed: Maximum 4 events.
Groups A & B: From each serial-number row, a
student may choose only one event — either the
Group A event or the Group B event in that row. 
(e.g, Between Harmony Hustle and Groove-o-Mania,
one participant may choose only one event not both)
Group C: A student may participate in Group C events
without crossing the overall maximum participation
limit.
Group D: A student may participate in any 2
submission-based contests in addition to the
permitted live-event participation.
College-level participant limits for each competition
are given in the event-specific section below
PARTICIPATION GUIDELINES

=== PAGE 5 ===
Registration: All entries must be submitted within the       
registration window communicated by the organisers.
Late or on-spot registrations will not be accepted unless
specifically permitted by the organisers.
Participants must carry valid college/student
identification and report at the venue at least 30
minutes before the scheduled start of their event.
Participants are responsible for carrying all materials,
devices, props, costumes and other permitted
requirements specified for their competition.
Obscene, vulgar, sexually explicit, hateful, religious,
discriminatory or otherwise inappropriate content is
strictly prohibited.
Audio tracks, presentation files and digital submissions
must comply with the formats and deadlines specified
by the organisers.
Entries that violate competition rules, submission
requirements or event discipline may be disqualified.
The decision of the judging panel shall be final and
binding.
Participants must follow venue instructions, maintain
discipline and leave competition spaces clean and
orderly.

14. SPELLBOUND (SPELL BEE)
• Entries:  5 solo participants per college; at least 1 must be female.
• Format: Individual competition conducted over four rounds.
Round 1 — Dictation Round
•Twenty words will be dictated to all participants.
•The top 30 participants, based on their scores, will qualify for Round 2.
Round 2 — Moderate-Level Spell Bee
•Each participant will pick a chit containing a word and spell it aloud.
•Participants will have a maximum of two attempts.
•A correct first attempt earns 2 marks; a correct second attempt earns 1
mark.
Round 3 — Advanced Spell Bee
•Participants will identify and spell a target word used in a sentence.
•One attempt is permitted per word.
Round 4 — Finale
•The finale will follow a rapid-fire elimination format.
•Each finalist must spell the given word correctly in one attempt. An incorrect
spelling results in elimination.
•The final winner will be determined through performance in the elimination
round.
Additional rules:
•Words may be drawn from general English usage,
literature, culture, science, arts and academic
vocabulary.
•Participants must spell each announced word
clearly.
•The moderator may ask for the word’s meaning,
pronunciation or use in a sentence.
•Mobile phones, dictionaries, internet access and
external assistance are prohibited.
•The moderator’s decision regarding pronunciation,
spelling, scoring and elimination shall be final.
Judging Criteria
•Spelling Accuracy
•Pronunciation
•Vocabulary Knowledge
• Composure and Response Time

•Obscene, vulgar, sexually explicit or offensive content is
strictly prohibited.
•Participants may use voice, expression and performance
techniques. Elaborate stage setups are not permitted.
Judging Criteria
•Originality and Writing
•Theme and Idea
•Language and Imagery
•Voice and Expression
•Stage Presence

•Hindi and English will be conducted as separate language

and Final Round.

17. LIT CHECK (LITERATURE QUIZ)
•Entries: One team of four students per college.
•The competition will consist of four rounds.
Round 1 — Questioning Round: Questions related to authors, poets,
books, literary movements and famous literary works.
Round 2 — Audio Round: Identification of literary personalities, works,
characters or references through audio clips.
Round 3 — Visual Round: Identification of authors, books, characters,
literary symbols or references through visual clues.
Round 4 — Rapid-Fire Round: Teams answer as many questions as
possible within the allotted time.
